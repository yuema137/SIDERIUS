# PaperExtract Compression — Pilot (Behavioral Checkpoint B)

**Artifact for**: Commit 3 of [`commit_plan_ml_literature_review.md`](commit_plan_ml_literature_review.md),
Behavioral Checkpoint B ("Single paper LLM compression quality").

**What this decides**: whether the finalised `PaperExtract` schema and the
`render_paper_extract_prompt` compression prompt produce extracts specific
enough to influence architectural proposals — i.e. a model proposer reading
**only** the `PaperExtract` (never the source paper) learns something concrete,
correct, and actionable.

**Run date**: 2026-05-26 · **Prompt**: `agent/prompt_templates/literature_review/paper_extract_system.md`
· **Compression model**: `openai / gpt-4o-mini` · **Full text via**:
`paper_resolver_skill` (verbosity=2, live S2 + arxiv PDF).

**Hard requirement (domain correctness)**: the compression must qualify every
model performance comparison with its training regime. TIDMAD benchmarks models
under **frequency-split** training; SIDERIUS trains **full-spectrum**. WaveNet is
the only full-spectrum baseline. An extract that ranks FCNet/PUNet/Transformer/RNN
without the frequency-split qualifier is a **failed compression** (pilot finding
F4). This was verified — see §3.

## 4. Prompt revision made at Checkpoint B

The first TIDMAD run met the hard requirement in `architecture_details` but had
two gaps: `key_results` quoted the headline score (`6.43`) **without** its
training regime, and `architecture_details` listed model *families* rather than
mechanisms. One targeted revision was applied (the approved verbatim
frequency-split block was left untouched; the instructions were added at the
field definitions instead):

1. `key_results` — "Attach the training regime to EVERY performance number you
   report (e.g. 'scored 6.43 under frequency-split training'); a bare score with
   no regime is misleading."
2. `architecture_details` — "If the paper benchmarks multiple models, describe
   the concrete mechanism (layer types, key design choices) of the architectures
   most relevant to the task — not just their family names."

### Before / after (TIDMAD)

| Field | Before revision | After revision |
|---|---|---|
| `key_results` | "…The best-performing deep learning model achieved a denoising score of **6.43**, significantly enhancing the signal recovery…" — no regime, no model named | "**The FC-Net model scored 6.43** on the denoising benchmark **under frequency-split training**, outperforming traditional methods…" |
| `architecture_details` | "…deep learning models such as FC-Net and WaveNet. … while many models were trained with frequency-split approaches, WaveNet effectively handled the full spectral range…" | "…including **FC-Net, WaveNet, and PU-Net**, each tailored to different frequency ranges. … frequency splitting, requiring multiple models trained on specific bands, while the **WaveNet architecture managed to process all frequencies effectively**. The FC-Net architecture notably achieved the highest denoising scores." |
| `year` | "2025" (v3 arxiv stamp) | "2024" (correct original) |

The revision is locked by a unit assertion
(`test_system_prompt_has_checkpoint_b_revision_instructions`).
